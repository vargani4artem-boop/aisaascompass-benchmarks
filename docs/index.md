## 1. Commercial Fleet AI Dash Cams: Samsara Native CM32 vs Geotab Marketplace Cameras

Commercial fleet safety directors evaluating dual-facing AI dash cams frequently uncover an architectural split between native first-party camera ecosystems and open OBD-II telematics marketplaces.

- **Samsara (CM31 / CM32 Dual-Facing HD):** First-party hardware with on-device Edge AI voice coaching (cell phone detection, tailgating, rolling stops) bundled at `$57–$68/truck/month` inside a single cloud dashboard.
- **Geotab (GO9 + Third-Party Marketplace Cameras):** Geotab does not manufacture dash cam hardware; fleets pair the GO9 telematics gateway with partner cameras (`Lytx Surfsight`, `Rosco`, `Streamax`) plus separate cellular video data plans (`$32 + $35–$48/truck/month`).

## 2. Veterinary Ambient AI SOAP Note-Taking: Digitail TailAI vs ezyVet Third-Party Scribe Integrations

Veterinary practice owners and medical directors comparing cloud Practice Information Management Systems (PIMS) evaluate ambient voice-to-SOAP documentation costs per full-time veterinarian (DVM):

- **Digitail (`TailAI` Native Voice-to-SOAP):** Embedded natively inside the Digitail mobile and web clinical workflow (`$0–$39/DVM/month`), automatically generating structured Subjective/Objective/Assessment/Plan notes and suggesting missed invoice charges.
- **ezyVet (Third-Party AI Scribe Add-Ons):** Requires pairing enterprise hospital workflows with external AI dictation apps (`Scribenote`, `VetRec`, or `CoVet`) at `+$99 to +$199/DVM/month`.
- **Interactive Model:** Compare multi-DVM 3-year costs with and without AI SOAP scribes in the [Digitail vs ezyVet AI Note-Taking & Veterinary PIMS TCO Calculator](https://aisaascompass.com/articles/ezyvet-vs-shepherd-vs-digitail-vet-calculator/).

## 3. Heavy-Duty Truck Shop & Fleet Maintenance ERP: RTA Fleet360 vs Fleetio

Maintenance directors operating dedicated mechanic bays vs outsourced repair shop networks face distinct software trade-offs:

- **RTA Fleet360:** Engineered for in-house heavy diesel, transit, and municipal repair shops requiring full 9-digit VMRS coding, barcode parts-room bin replenishment, mechanic wrench-time clocks, and automated OEM component warranty recovery.
- **Fleetio:** Optimized for distributed fleets outsourcing 50%+ of maintenance via Fleetio's Maintenance Shop Network and rapid driver mobile DVIR workflows (`$5–$12/asset/month`).
